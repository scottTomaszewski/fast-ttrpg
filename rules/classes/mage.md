# The Mage

The Mage stands far back and throws the biggest spells in the game. She can
also freeze enemies in place, turn one into a sheep, hop out of trouble,
freeze herself in a block of ice, and call her mana back.

- **HP:** 28
- **Auto-attack:** d4, on an enemy up to
  10 squares away.
- **Resource:** mana. At most 30, you start a fight with
  30, and you get 1 back at the top of
  each party turn.
- **Crit bonus:** 2 mana.
- **Mana from a hit:** each auto-attack that does not miss gives you
  1 mana, crit or not.

## Abilities

### Arcane Volley

- 2 mana for each hit ·
  Channel, 3 hits · range
  10
- Hit one enemy for **2d6**, now and in each of
  your next act steps, for 3 hits in all. You
  may move while you channel. Each hit is paid for, and checks its range,
  when it happens.

### Firebolt

- 9 mana · Cast, 2 rounds · range
  10
- When it lands, hit one enemy for **6d6**.
- It is **all or nothing.** You start it in your act step and keep casting
  in your next one, and it lands at the top of the turn after that. Stand
  still. If you do anything else in the middle (Disrupt, Blink, Ice
  Block...), or you move, are stunned or go down, it is gone and does
  nothing. If its target dies first, it does nothing too. Either way you
  pay nothing.

### Meteor

- 10 mana · Cast, 2 rounds · range
  10
- Pick a square. Every square within 2 of it is
  marked when you start. When it lands, roll **8d6**
  once: every enemy in the area, and **every hero who is up in the area,
  you too**, takes all of it.

### Frost Nova

- 3 mana · Instant ·
  around you
- Every enemy next to you gets **2 rooted
  cards**: it cannot move, but it can still attack if someone is in reach.
  It works on a boss too.

### Blink

- 2 mana · Instant · range
  6
- Jump to an empty square, without walking there.

### Polymorph

- 4 mana · Cast, 1 round · range
  10
- When it lands, one enemy becomes a sheep: **12
  sheep cards**. It does nothing while it has them, but turns back the
  moment it takes any damage. Not on a boss. Only one sheep at a time:
  when a new Polymorph lands, any other sheep turns back.

### Ice Block

- 0 mana · Instant · yourself · cooldown
  8
- Freeze yourself in a block of ice: take **1
  Ice Block card**. While you hold it, nothing can hurt you (a blast or a
  heavy hit does you no harm, and nothing can stun you), no enemy can
  pick you as its target, and you cannot move or act. It comes off at the
  top of your next party turn, so it covers the GM turn that follows.
- Going into the ice takes **every bad card** off you (stun, poison,
  rooted and the like; not Bleeding, not cooldowns), and ends your own
  cast or channel.
- **You may use it while stunned:** it is the one thing a stunned hero
  can do.
- Then put 8 cooldown cards on Ice Block.

### Evocation

- 0 mana · Channel, 2 rounds · yourself ·
  cooldown 8
- Call your mana back: get **6 mana** now, and
  6 more in your next act step if you keep
  channelling (2 rounds in all, never more than
  30).
- **Stand still** while you channel it: moving ends it. Taking damage does
  not. Doing anything else, or being stunned, ends it too.
- Then put 8 cooldown cards on Evocation.

### Disrupt (every hero has this)

- 0 mana · Instant · range
  **10** for you (the others need to be next to the
  enemy) · cooldown 3
- Break the cast an enemy has announced, so it never lands. Only on an
  enemy that has announced one. Then put 3
  cooldown cards on Disrupt.

## How to play the Mage

- **Stand back.** Stay within 10 squares of your
  target, but well back from every enemy. Stand still while you cast.
- **Arcane Volley is your everyday spell.** You can move while it goes,
  and you can stop it part-way and keep the hits that already landed. The
  owner's words: "A mage can let the spell hit twice and then cancel the
  cast to skip the third hit to kick off another, more urgent spell or
  interrupt."
- **Firebolt when you have time and mana to spare.** It does as much as a
  whole Volley in two of your act steps instead of three, but it costs
  more and it is all or nothing. Start one only when you can pay for it and still have a whole
  Volley's mana left, and no enemy could walk up to you before your next
  turn (an enemy held by the Guardian, or with Taunt cards on it, will not).
  If something needs you in the middle, let the Firebolt go and do it.
- **You are the best at Disrupt:** yours reaches 10
  squares. Break the shaman's mend first, then the boss's big moves, even
  in the middle of a Volley or a Firebolt.
- **Something reached you?** Frost Nova an add next to you. If it is the
  boss, Blink to a square it cannot reach.
- **Ice Block is your last way out.** Use it when a big hit is coming at
  you that could take you to 0 and you cannot Disrupt it or walk out of it.
  Also when you are at half HP or less, the boss or an elite is next to you
  and after you, and you cannot Blink away. It works even while stunned.
- **Sheep the biggest add nobody is fighting** (an add is an extra enemy
  that fights alongside the boss), when there are at least two adds. Then
  leave it, and any sapped enemy, alone until nothing else is left: a hit
  wakes it.
- **Meteor** a spot that covers three or more enemies, where no sapped or
  sheeped enemy stands, and that your allies can walk out of.
- **Low on mana and nobody needs you?** With less than a whole Volley's
  mana, use Evocation: stand still and get it back. Not while an enemy is
  next to you, a blast is coming down on you, a cast needs your Disrupt,
  or the boss has no Taunt cards.
- **Who to hit:** the enemy casting a mend first, then the add with the
  least HP, and the boss last. Out of mana? Auto-attack: each hit gives
  some back.
